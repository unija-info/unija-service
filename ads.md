# Unija Runner — Platform Benefits

## For Students (Customers)

**Find the right runner in seconds.**

- **Live status** — green pulse dot shows who's available right now, before you even message
- **Verified badges** — only runners approved by the platform carry the Verified Runner label
- **Full profile before you commit** — see availability hours, coverage area, tags, and full price list before opening WhatsApp
- **One tap to order** — WhatsApp button pre-fills your message, no copy-pasting numbers
- **Search by name or service** — type what you need, results filter instantly
- **UniParcels runners flagged** — need parcel delivery specifically? Spot them at a glance

---

## For Runners

**Your own professional profile. Zero setup.**

- **Personal profile link** — share `yoursite.com/profile?id=YOUR-ID` anywhere: WhatsApp bio, Instagram, sticker on your bag
- **Service receipt built in** — write your price list once in the sheet, it appears as a formatted receipt on your profile — no designing, no PDF
- **Control your own status** — go online or offline anytime by submitting a form, no need to message the admin
- **Show your availability** — list your operating hours so customers know exactly when to reach you
- **Coverage area visible** — specify your kawasan so you only get relevant orders
- **Tags for discoverability** — customers searching for "Laundry" or "24 Jam" will find you

---

## For the Platform (Admin)

**Run a full directory with almost no overhead.**

- **Google Sheets as the CMS** — add, update, or remove runners from a familiar spreadsheet. No dashboard to learn.
- **Auto-sync every 5 minutes** — edit the sheet, live site updates in under 5 minutes. Fully automated.
- **Runners manage their own status** — the PIN system lets runners toggle online/offline without messaging you. Scales to 50+ runners with no extra work.
- **Zero hosting cost** — static files on GitHub Pages. No server, no database, no monthly bill.
- **No code required to update content** — everything runners need to change (status, availability, price list) flows through Google Sheets or a Google Form.
- **Reliable sync** — cron-job.org guarantees the 5-minute trigger even when GitHub's own scheduler lags.
